# Latching (F71): tried, measured, not kept (section 35)

Asked, in the owner's words: thoughts do not grow by focus; the path is the path of thoughts; the mind locks onto
the right thought and widens it; silent claims should work like that.

What was built (`latch.diff` on commit 0dd75f9, `test_zihin_latch.py`): while the mind only listens, a run of items
(an utterance, a sentence) is one thought. It is sought from the run's beginning until it claims something,
followed to the next context while what it claimed is what was then heard, and with every claim that held the
window of F70 is one step wider; where a claim of it fails it is let go, and the mind only listens until the next
run begins. Off: the reading is made at every context the mind has no record of (silent claims as they are).

Every world was the same. On running text a fifth of the readings were not made, and the cost a moment did not
fall: the readings it let go were the cheap ones (the beginning of a failed thought), and those it followed are the
ones that cost (a full reading 41 ms against 27). It lost one exact and one valid answer of a hundred. Nothing it
was meant to bring was measured, so it is not part of the mind.
