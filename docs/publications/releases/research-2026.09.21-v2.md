# Cevahir 2026.09.21.2 — Academic Book Expansion

**Author: Muhammed Yasin Yılmaz**

This edition expands the Turkish technical book's neural-network, embedding, attention, Transformer and training chapters. It connects mathematical derivations and original publications to actual Cevahir files, methods, configuration choices and executed examples. The book remains an evolving educational manuscript; the release does not claim whole-book completion or external peer review.

- Twelve chapters; 98 second-level and 50 third-level headings in chapter files.
- Forty bibliography entries across the four expanded chapters, corresponding to 36 distinct primary publications and official documentation pages.
- Twenty-two solved exercises; seven executed CPU example groups, including MHA/MQA/GQA cache checks and an in-memory small-model update.
- Ninety-nine selected neural-core, attention/cache, MoE and training tests passed. This is not a GPU, language-quality or whole-repository validation claim.
- All nine research report source records and prior negative findings are preserved. Report-edition headers identify the new collection version; their research results are unchanged.
