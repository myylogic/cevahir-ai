# Insufficiency witnesses in representation search: deterministic equivalence and a negative result

**Muhammed Yasin Yılmaz**

CEV-2026-02 · Version 2026.11.24 · Published 2026-11-24

Research report / preprint. Not peer reviewed.

## Abstract

Witness filtering and direct representation search are equivalent when they share candidate language, order and complete deterministic consistency checking. All 15 finite comparisons yield identical selected representations, predictions and abstentions. Filtering changes some search costs but provides no additional learning information under this contract.

## Interpretation of the findings

The hypothesis that witness filtering gives a new learning advantage under these conditions was eliminated; the negative result is kept.

The result makes no claim of superiority or equivalence for other information access, open-ended expression generation, approximate consistency or truncated computation budgets.
