# Persistent procedures, transfer and retesting: first separations in learning while living

**Muhammed Yasin Yılmaz**

CEV-2026-03 · Version 2026.11.24 · Published 2026-11-24

Research report / preprint. Not peer reviewed.

## Abstract

Finite affine worlds separate persistence, transfer and budgeted execution of experience-derived procedures. A strong raw-history baseline with the same inference capability matches accuracy. Transferred adaptation knowledge helps within a family and can hurt under shift. Closed-loop retesting is examined as one subproblem rather than a general solution to lifelong learning.

## Interpretation of the findings

A persistent right answer, inference cost, update transfer and benefit under change are not the same measure; strong baselines limit many first interpretations of superiority.

The function language and the environment families are given. The expected benefit of retesting does not solve the acquisition of a new computational language or general controlled learning.
