# Update behaviour acquired from experience: the benefit of transfer and its reversal

**Muhammed Yasin Yılmaz**

CEV-2026-06 · Version 2026.11.24 · Published 2026-11-24

Research report / preprint. Not peer reviewed.

## Abstract

An update matrix learned across source lives is transferred while prediction weights are reset. Identical initial predictions can therefore lead to different future learning curves. Transfer helps in the same geometry and hurts under reversal; continued adaptation only partly repairs the damage. A separate moment-based risk calculation checks the interpretation.

## Interpretation of the findings

Experience can change how learning will happen in the future; this capacity cannot be measured from a fixed initial answer, and it can carry its own harm under distribution change.

A small linear geometry and an externally given meta-update family are used. Because it matches IDBD and related literature, no new general learning principle is claimed.
